# What to expect

1. Deadlines
	1. Most programs only have fall terms (start in august)
2. Academic Transcripts
	1. Minimum 3.0 GPA
	2. Bachelor's Degree
	3. Required Coursework
3. Personal Statement (letter)
4. CV
5. Letter(s) of Recommendation
6. Standardized tests
	1. English profficiency (IELTS)

# Application Tips

 - Academic Resume (Don't be humble, BRAG ABOUT YOURSELF)
	 - Education
	 - Research Experience
	 - Teaching
 - Personal Statement
	 - Connect to the Department's faculty and research activity
	 - Be clear on WHY UofA (they want some boot-licking)
 - Letters of Recommendation
	 - Plan for 3 letters (Ask them with time)
	 - Ask both academic and professional connections (if related)
